## The short version
Texting lacks non-verbal cues, leading to misinterpretations, but intentional strategies like specific language, emotional mirroring, and careful punctuation can foster deep connection. Use text for maintenance and light intimacy, but prefer voice or video for conflict resolution.

## Why it matters
Understanding the limitations and strengths of digital communication helps prevent misunderstandings and allows individuals to build meaningful relationships despite physical distance. Recognizing when to switch communication modes can reduce anxiety and improve relationship quality.

## Try this today
- Practice naming emotions explicitly and using 'I feel' statements to clarify intent in text messages.
- Match the emotional tone and length of your responses to the vulnerability displayed by the recipient.
- Switch to voice or video calls for resolving conflicts or discussing serious emotional topics.

## Limitations
Text cannot replicate the biological synchrony of in-person interaction. It is prone to misinterpretation, especially during conflicts or when the recipient is anxious. The effectiveness of emojis and punctuation varies by individual and cultural context.
