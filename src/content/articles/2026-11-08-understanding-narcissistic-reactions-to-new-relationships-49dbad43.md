## The short version
Narcissistic reactions to an ex’s new relationship stem from ego threats and a need for control, not love. Behaviors like rage or love bombing aim to reassert dominance. Survivors are advised to use non-engagement, set boundaries, and seek professional help to manage trauma and protect new relationships.

## Why it matters
Understanding that these reactions are rooted in the narcissist’s self-image rather than the survivor’s worth helps reduce self-blame and emotional reactivity. Recognizing specific manipulation tactics allows individuals to set effective boundaries and seek appropriate support, which is crucial for healing and maintaining healthy future relationships.

## Try this today
- Practice non-engagement by providing neutral, brief responses to any necessary contact to avoid fueling the narcissist’s need for reaction.
- Document any escalating behavior, such as repeated unwanted contact or threats, to support potential legal or safety measures.
- Consult a qualified mental health professional experienced in narcissistic abuse to address trauma bonding and rebuild trust in new relationships.
