## The short version
ADHD founder coaching offers business-focused accountability and systems but is not a substitute for clinical diagnosis or medication. Costs range from roughly $140 to $345 monthly for apps, while independent coaches often lack transparent pricing. Evidence for effectiveness is limited, and coaching is best for those with existing diagnoses seeking operational support.

## Why it matters
Founders with ADHD often struggle with follow-through and operational consistency, which can impact business viability. Understanding the specific scope of coaching versus clinical care helps individuals allocate resources effectively and avoid seeking inappropriate support for medical needs.

## Try this today
- Determine if you have a formal ADHD diagnosis; if not, consult a licensed clinician for evaluation before considering coaching.
- If seeking coaching, request written pricing and verify credentials such as ICF accreditation to ensure transparency and professional standards.
- Review the original article for specific provider details and discuss your specific business challenges with a qualified professional to determine if coaching is a suitable support tool.
