## The short version
ADHD relationship coaching in the Washpark area is often provided by local therapy practices or national virtual platforms. Coaching is not therapy or diagnosis; it focuses on skills and accountability. Verify if a provider is a licensed therapist or independent coach, and ensure you are not seeking diagnosis or crisis care, which require clinical professionals.

## Why it matters
Understanding the distinction between coaching and clinical therapy prevents misallocation of resources and ensures individuals receive appropriate care. Coaching can support daily management of ADHD-related friction in relationships, but it cannot address underlying medical diagnoses or severe mental health crises. Clarifying the provider's credentials and scope helps avoid confusion and potential financial loss.

## Try this today
- Verify in writing whether a local provider is a licensed therapist or an independent coach before booking.
- If seeking diagnosis, medication management, or crisis support, consult a qualified clinician or call the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
- Review refund policies and ask about ICF accreditation when evaluating virtual coaching platforms.
