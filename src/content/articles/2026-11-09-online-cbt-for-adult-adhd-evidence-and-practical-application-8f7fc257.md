## The short version
Online CBT for adult ADHD builds practical skills like planning and self-monitoring. Evidence shows it improves core symptoms and related anxiety, though medication remains the most effective short-term treatment overall.

## Why it matters
Understanding the specific skills-based nature of CBT helps individuals distinguish it from medication management services. It provides a non-pharmacological option for managing executive function challenges and associated emotional distress in adults.

## Try this today
- Verify that a prospective online provider offers specific CBT or behavioral skills sessions rather than just medication management.
- Prepare for initial sessions by identifying specific symptoms and goals, such as missed deadlines or task initiation difficulties.
- Consult a qualified healthcare provider for diagnosis and to discuss whether combining therapy with medication is appropriate for your specific symptom severity.

## Limitations
Online assessment may miss physical symptoms like restlessness, potentially requiring multiple visits for diagnosis. CBT is not a standalone fix for all cases; medication is often more effective for severe symptoms. The evidence cited is primarily for adults, and findings may not apply to children.
