They are often considered when stimulants are contraindicated due to cardiac issues or substance use history. Prescribing requires a clinical evaluation, which may involve in-person visits depending on state laws. While generic substitution is common, release mechanisms can differ between brands and generics, potentially affecting how the medication feels. ## The short version
Generic ADHD meds are cheaper but identical in active ingredients to brands. Stimulants are controlled substances with faster onset and higher dependency risks, while non-stimulants like atomoxetine are not controlled and take longer to work. Proper diagnosis and provider consultation are essential for safe use.

## Why it matters
Understanding the difference between stimulant and non-stimulant classes helps patients make informed decisions about cost, side effects, and safety. Knowing that generics are FDA-approved equivalents can reduce unnecessary anxiety about brand-name pricing, while recognizing the specific risks of each class ensures safer treatment choices.

## Try this today
- Consult a qualified healthcare provider for a proper ADHD evaluation and medication prescription.
- Discuss specific medical history, such as cardiac conditions or substance use, with a prescriber to determine if stimulants are appropriate.
- Review current medications and supplements with a pharmacist or doctor to check for potential interactions before starting new treatment.
