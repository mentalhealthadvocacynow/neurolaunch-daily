## The short version
New data suggests ADHD meds may be safe in pregnancy, but no drug is FDA-approved for this use. Consult an OB and prescriber for personalized care.

## Why it matters
Understanding the distinction between 'no evidence of harm' and 'proven safe' is critical for making informed decisions about medication management during pregnancy, balancing the risks of untreated ADHD against potential medication side effects.

## Try this today
- Consult with an obstetrician or maternal-fetal medicine specialist to coordinate any ADHD medication decisions.
- Discuss the risks of abrupt medication discontinuation with a prescriber before making changes.
- Consider screening for thyroid or nutrient deficiencies if experiencing new inattention or fatigue.

## Limitations
