## The short version
The TOVA is a paid, clinic-administered attention test that supports but does not replace a clinical diagnosis. It costs $150–$580+, and scores must be interpreted by a professional. Free self-report tools like the ASRS are better for initial screening.

## Why it matters
Understanding the TOVA’s role helps individuals avoid misinterpreting test scores as definitive diagnoses. It clarifies that the test is one component of a broader evaluation, preventing unnecessary anxiety or false reassurance. Knowing the costs and the lack of at-home options allows for better financial and logistical planning when seeking professional care.

## Try this today
- Take the free ASRS v1.1 self-report screener to determine if further clinical evaluation is warranted.
- Consult a qualified healthcare provider to interpret any TOVA scores in the context of a full diagnostic workup.
- Verify insurance coverage and total costs for both the TOVA and the accompanying clinical evaluation before booking an appointment.

## Limitations
The TOVA does not diagnose ADHD on its own and is subject to false positives or negatives influenced by sleep, anxiety, or motivation. Scores are norm-based and require professional interpretation.
