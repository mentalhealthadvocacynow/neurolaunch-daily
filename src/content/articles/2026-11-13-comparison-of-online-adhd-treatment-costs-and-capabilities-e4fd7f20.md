## The short version
Klarity is the only platform among Klarity, Brightside, and Hims that treats ADHD, with self-pay visits from $51. Brightside and Hims do not offer ADHD care. Klarity carries risks regarding prescription fulfillment and provider variability.

## Why it matters
Many online mental health services do not treat ADHD, leading to confusion for patients seeking stimulant prescriptions. Understanding which platforms actually offer ADHD care and the associated costs and risks is essential for making informed decisions about treatment access.
