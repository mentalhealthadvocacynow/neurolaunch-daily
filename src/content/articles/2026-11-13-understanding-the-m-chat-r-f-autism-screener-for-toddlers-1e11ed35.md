## The short version
The M-CHAT-R/F is a free, validated screener for toddlers aged 16-30 months that flags potential autism risks but does not provide a diagnosis. Positive or medium results require follow-up with a qualified clinician for a formal evaluation.

## Why it matters
Early identification of developmental concerns allows for timely access to interventions. Understanding the distinction between screening and diagnosis helps parents navigate the healthcare system effectively and avoid misinterpreting preliminary results as final medical conclusions.

## Try this today
- Complete the free M-CHAT-R/F screener at the official author site if your child is between 16 and 30 months old.
- Consult a pediatrician or qualified healthcare provider to discuss any screening results or observed developmental concerns.
- Seek immediate professional advice if a child experiences a loss of previously acquired language or social skills.

## Limitations
The M-CHAT-R/F is a parent-report tool and does not replace a clinical diagnosis. It is only validated for the 16-30 month age range.
