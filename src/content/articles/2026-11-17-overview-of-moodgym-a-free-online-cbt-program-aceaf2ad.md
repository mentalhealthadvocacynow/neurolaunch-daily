## The short version
MoodGym is a free, self-guided online CBT tool for mild-to-moderate depression and anxiety. It offers structured modules and mood tracking but is not suitable for severe mental health crises.

## Why it matters
A significant portion of people with mental health conditions do not receive treatment due to cost or access barriers. MoodGym provides an accessible, low-cost option that can bridge this gap for those with mild-to-moderate symptoms who cannot access traditional therapy.

## Try this today
- Consider using MoodGym as a self-guided resource if you are experiencing mild-to-moderate symptoms and have access to the internet.
- Engage consistently with the program's exercises and mood-tracking tools rather than passively reading the content.
- Seek immediate professional help or contact a crisis line if you experience severe symptoms, thoughts of self-harm, or psychosis, as MoodGym is not a crisis intervention.

## Limitations
MoodGym is not appropriate for severe depression, active suicidality, psychosis, or complex trauma. It lacks a human therapeutic relationship, which is a key predictor of therapy success. Completion rates for self-guided programs are low, and the program requires independent motivation. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
