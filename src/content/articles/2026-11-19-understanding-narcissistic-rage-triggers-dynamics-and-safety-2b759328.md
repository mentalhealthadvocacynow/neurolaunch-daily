## The short version
Narcissistic rage is a disproportionate response to perceived threats to self-image, driven by underlying shame. It differs from normal anger by seeking domination rather than resolution. Victims often face gaslighting and trauma, requiring professional support and safety planning for recovery.

## Why it matters
Understanding the mechanics of narcissistic rage helps individuals recognize that their reactions are not personal failures but defensive mechanisms of the other party. This knowledge is crucial for validating one's experience, reducing self-blame, and identifying when a relationship has become unsafe. It also highlights the importance of professional intervention for both the individual exhibiting these traits and the victims suffering from trauma.

## Try this today
- If you are in a situation involving physical violence or imminent threat, prioritize safety planning and contact a domestic violence resource immediately.
- Consider seeking support from a qualified mental health professional who specializes in trauma or personality disorders to process experiences and rebuild self-worth.
- Maintain a record of incidents and build a support network outside the relationship to counter isolation and gaslighting.
