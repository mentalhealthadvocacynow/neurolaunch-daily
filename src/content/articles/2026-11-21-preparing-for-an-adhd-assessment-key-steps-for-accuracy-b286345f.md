## The short version
Prepare for an ADHD assessment by gathering childhood records, logging specific symptoms for weeks, and involving family members for collateral reports. Do not change medications without medical advice.

## Why it matters
Thorough preparation reduces the risk of misdiagnosis or missed diagnosis by providing clinicians with reliable historical data and concrete behavioral evidence, which is essential for meeting diagnostic criteria that require childhood onset and multi-setting symptoms.

## Try this today
- Gather old school report cards, previous psychological records, and a complete list of current medications and supplements to bring to the appointment.
- Start a detailed symptom log two to four weeks before the assessment, noting specific examples of difficulties in work, relationships, and daily tasks.
- Contact a parent, sibling, or long-term partner to provide collateral information about childhood behavior and current functioning, and ask the clinician about their policy on involving informants.
