## The short version
Optimize your home office for ADHD by adding controlled background noise, bright lighting, and visible organizational systems to support focus and reduce cognitive load.

## Why it matters
Understanding the neurological basis of ADHD attention allows individuals to create environments that work with their brain's needs, potentially improving productivity and reducing the stress associated with working from home.

## Try this today
- Position your desk near a window to access natural light and provide a low-stakes visual resting point for your eyes.
- Implement a visible organization system, such as open shelving or color-coded folders, to keep priorities in view and reduce reliance on working memory.
- Experiment with moderate background noise, such as white noise or ambient music, to maintain optimal arousal levels during work sessions.
