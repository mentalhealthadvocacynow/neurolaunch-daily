## The short version
ADHD Combined Type involves both inattention and hyperactivity. It is a neurodevelopmental condition with delayed brain maturation. Diagnosis requires specific criteria, and treatment typically combines medication with behavioral strategies.

## Why it matters
Accurate diagnosis is crucial because symptoms can mimic anxiety or bipolar disorder, and women are often underdiagnosed due to internalized symptoms. Understanding the neurological basis helps reduce stigma and guides effective, multi-faceted treatment plans that address both core symptoms and co-occurring issues.

## Try this today
- Consult a qualified healthcare provider, such as a psychiatrist or neuropsychologist, for a comprehensive evaluation if symptoms cause significant impairment in multiple life areas.
- Consider implementing environmental structures, such as timers and routines, to support executive functions, and discuss the potential benefits of ADHD-adapted cognitive-behavioral therapy with a professional.
- Monitor for co-occurring conditions like anxiety or sleep disorders and ensure they are addressed as part of the overall treatment plan.
