## The short version
Denial of an autism diagnosis is a common grief response that delays critical early intervention. The quality of the initial diagnosis delivery significantly impacts how quickly families engage with support. Connecting with other parents and understanding the diagnosis as a tool for access to services can help families move forward.

## Why it matters
Early intervention during the first three years of life is crucial for developmental outcomes. Delays caused by parental denial or family conflict can lead to entrenched behavioral patterns and missed opportunities for support, affecting the child's long-term social and academic development.

## Try this today
- Request a detailed follow-up with the diagnostic team to clarify findings and address specific concerns about the evaluation process.
- Connect with peer support groups or other parents who have experienced similar journeys to gain perspective and reduce isolation.
- Consult with a qualified healthcare provider or therapist to address parental distress or family conflict that may be hindering the child's access to services.
