While APD has established clinical pathways, SPD lacks a standalone diagnostic code in the DSM-5, which can complicate insurance coverage and school accommodations. ## The short version
Processing disorders involve the brain's difficulty interpreting sensory input despite normal hearing or vision. Diagnosis requires comprehensive specialist testing to distinguish from conditions like ADHD, and treatment involves therapy and accommodations.

## Why it matters
Misdiagnosis is common because symptoms of processing disorders overlap with ADHD and other conditions. Accurate identification through proper testing ensures that interventions target the root cause, improving academic performance and reducing frustration for individuals and families.

## Try this today
- Consult an audiologist or occupational therapist for a comprehensive evaluation if sensory difficulties persist despite normal hearing and vision screenings.
- Gather school records and developmental history to share with specialists to aid in accurate diagnosis and differential assessment.

## Limitations
This summary is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. SPD lacks a formal DSM-5 diagnostic code, which may affect insurance coverage and official recognition. Individual outcomes vary, and professional guidance is essential for diagnosis and treatment planning.
