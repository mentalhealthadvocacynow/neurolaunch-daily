## The short version
Impulsivity in autism is a neurological trait affecting action inhibition and sensory processing, not a behavioral choice. It overlaps with ADHD and is best managed through environmental adjustments, sensory regulation, and therapy rather than punishment.

## Why it matters
Recognizing impulsivity as a biological mechanism rather than defiance prevents ineffective and harmful disciplinary approaches. Understanding the specific triggers, such as sensory overload or emotional regulation difficulties, allows for targeted support that improves quality of life and reduces distress for autistic individuals.

## Try this today
- Identify specific sensory or emotional triggers to adjust the environment and reduce the frequency of impulsive moments.
- Practice 'stop and think' scripts or social stories during calm periods to build rehearsed pause mechanisms.
- Consult a qualified healthcare provider, such as a developmental pediatrician or autism-specialized psychologist, if impulsivity leads to harm or significant functional strain.
