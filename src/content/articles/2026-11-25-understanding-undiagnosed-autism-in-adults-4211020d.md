## The short version
Autism is often missed in adults, especially women, due to historical diagnostic biases and effective masking. Recognizing signs like social exhaustion and sensory issues can lead to late diagnosis, which may provide relief and better support.

## Why it matters
Many adults live with undiagnosed autism, leading to unnecessary suffering, misdiagnosis of co-occurring conditions, and a lack of appropriate accommodations. Understanding the diverse presentations of autism helps reduce stigma and improves access to accurate support.

## Try this today
- Reflect on lifelong patterns of social exhaustion, sensory sensitivities, or the need to mask behavior to determine if a professional evaluation might be beneficial.
- Consult a qualified healthcare provider with specific experience in adult autism assessment if long-standing patterns interfere with daily functioning or mental health.
