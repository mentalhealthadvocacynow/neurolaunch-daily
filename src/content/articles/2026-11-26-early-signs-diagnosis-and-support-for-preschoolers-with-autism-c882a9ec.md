Early indicators include differences in social communication, such as reduced eye contact or a lack of joint attention, alongside repetitive behaviors and sensory processing differences. Evidence-based interventions, such as the Early Start Denver Model and modern, ethically grounded Applied Behavior Analysis, show that early support during peak neuroplasticity leads to lasting improvements in language, social skills, and adaptive behavior. ## The short version
Early signs of autism include social communication delays and sensory sensitivities. Prompt evaluation and evidence-based early intervention during the preschool years significantly improve long-term developmental outcomes.

## Why it matters
The preschool years represent a critical window of neuroplasticity. Delaying support can result in missed opportunities for skill development, while early intervention helps build foundational social and communication skills that persist into later childhood.

## Try this today
- Consult a pediatrician for a developmental screening if you notice delays in speech, social interaction, or play.
- Request a referral to early intervention services or a comprehensive autism evaluation team.
- Implement low-risk environmental supports at home, such as visual schedules and sensory-friendly adjustments.
