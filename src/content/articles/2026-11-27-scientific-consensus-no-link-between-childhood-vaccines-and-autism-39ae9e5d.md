## The short version
Extensive global research confirms vaccines do not cause autism. The original 1998 study linking them was retracted for fraud. Autism is primarily genetic, and vaccine timing coincides with symptom onset, not causation.

## Why it matters
Misinformation about vaccine safety leads to lower vaccination rates, which can cause outbreaks of preventable diseases like measles. These outbreaks disproportionately harm individuals who cannot be vaccinated due to age or medical conditions, undermining herd immunity and public health safety.

## Try this today
- Consult a pediatrician or developmental specialist for any concerns about a child's development or vaccination schedule.
- Review official guidance from national health authorities, such as the CDC, for accurate and current information on vaccine safety.
