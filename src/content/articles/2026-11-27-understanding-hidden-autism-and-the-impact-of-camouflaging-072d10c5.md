## The short version
Hidden autism occurs when individuals mask their traits to fit in, leading to higher rates of anxiety and burnout. Due to diagnostic biases, many adults, particularly women, go undiagnosed until later in life. Recognizing signs like social exhaustion and sensory issues can help individuals seek appropriate support and accommodations.

## Why it matters
Recognizing hidden autism is crucial because the very skills used to mask traits often prevent individuals from receiving necessary support, leading to severe mental health consequences. Understanding the distinction between personality quirks and neurodevelopmental differences allows for more accurate self-understanding and access to tailored accommodations that improve quality of life.

## Try this today
- Reflect on lifelong patterns of social exhaustion, sensory sensitivities, and the need for routine to identify potential signs of hidden autism.
- Consider discussing these patterns with a qualified healthcare professional who specializes in adult autism or neurodevelopmental conditions.
- Explore low-risk environmental adjustments, such as using noise-canceling headphones or requesting written follow-ups for verbal conversations, to reduce daily stress.

## Limitations
This summary is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Self-assessment is imperfect and cannot replace a formal clinical evaluation. Individual experiences vary, and the presence of certain traits does not automatically indicate autism. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for personal medical concerns.
